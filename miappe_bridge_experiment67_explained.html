Classification rules

  • Plant vs line: RIL 83 is a BiologicalContext, while each physical plant is a Sample. Measurements attach to the plant.
  • Nickel vs watering: the spike is the Factor, each watering is an Event, and the weights are Measurements.
  • Replicate: it belongs to the design and is not a physical block, because each tray mixes 3 to 5 replicates.
  • Position: tray, tray area and row (24 to 26) describe where the Observation Unit sits. They are not Environment.
  • Instrument settings: scanning height, rotation and device fields belong to the Measurement method.
  • Non-variable "trait" columns: qc_flags and pixel bounding-box positions are QC or geometry.

Items to resolve

  1. Confirm the treatment: nickel compound, µmol/L unit, and when and how it was applied. No dosing date exists.
  2. Fill study metadata: people, aims, location, growth facility, growing conditions and seed source.
  3. Propagate design and plant fields: only msc1 has design values and plant or tray metadata, so other sensors must join on plant_id. plant-weights.csv also has plant and tray fields.
  4. Confirm line identities: especially Ecm29, 9, 14 and 16, and how the RIL lines relate to genotype data.
  5. Check area calibration: area_mm2 peaks near 110 and plant width near 14 mm, which looks small. Treat area as relative until confirmed.
  6. Handle data quality: 54,454 missing crop-coordinate errors, 54,454 missing ViT-mask warnings, about 20% blank RGB area and Fv/Fm values, 7 plants with no vnir data, and bad weights (6 at 0 g, one at 1,004.1 g).
  7. Reconcile watering: the OPAL load report says watering mass is unavailable, but this export has before and after weights for 20,730 weighings.
